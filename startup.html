The Builder’s Path

My Startup Journey

I became interested in startups during my second year, created ATTENT-IN, a Student OS, and continued building from real problems I encountered.

I have moderate coding skills and am continuing to learn. My hands-on experience helped me understand the challenges builders face and influenced my decision to create ADYK Multi Venture Company.

Building hands-on taught me that successful products don’t rely solely on theory or assumptions—they emerge from directly confronting friction, observing authentic behavior, and making disciplined, evidence-based improvements.

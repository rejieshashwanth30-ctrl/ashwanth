Hands-On Building & Engineering

Every big thing I’ve built started with a question. I didn’t enter college with a startup plan. In my second year, curiosity led me to create ATTENT-IN, a Student OS—and showed me what can happen when an idea gets a real chance.

I’m not a coding expert, but I can build at a moderate level. Working through problems myself helped me understand the challenges people face when building products. That hands-on perspective is one reason I started ADYK Multi Venture.

On September 5, 2026, my friend Dinesh and I founded ADYK. In our first two months, we created ADYK Inspire and ADYK Cardex to work on real problems faced by developers and creators.

Then came a question I couldn’t ignore: how could I know whether the products I was building were working as they should? That question became ADYK WIQN, a software platform I’m building with useful additional features and bigger ambitions.

“I’m learning with every project, building with purpose, and only at the beginning of this journey.”

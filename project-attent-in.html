ATTENT-IN

My early Student OS project and a foundational step in my journey as a builder.

Overview

ATTENT-IN is a Student OS project created by Rejiesh Ashwanth. Conceptualized as an operating system concept designed around student needs, ATTENT-IN holds a meaningful place in my development as a hands-on builder.

It served as my initial bridge between classroom study and active software creation, representing the moment I committed to learning through direct execution.

Why I built it

I didn’t enter college with a concrete startup plan. During my second year studying AI & ML at Sri Krishna College of Engineering and Technology, curiosity about startups and how software companies get started inspired me to build.

I created ATTENT-IN to test my abilities, turn curiosity into action, and understand the practical difficulties of building a complete software system. Creating it gave me the confidence to keep building, laying the foundation for the subsequent platforms and tools I develop today.

Project links & Status

ATTENT-IN was an early exploratory venture that sparked my builder trajectory. As a foundational academic-year milestone, it provided the technical and founder confidence that fueled all future ventures.

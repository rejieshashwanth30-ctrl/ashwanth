Hackathon Project Built with My Team

ROAD SOS

My first hackathon project, built collaboratively with my team under intensive problem-solving conditions.

Overview

ROAD SOS was my first hackathon project, created collaboratively with my team. Designed and built under rapid sprint conditions, it served as our introduction to team-based software engineering and competitive development.

Working on ROAD SOS gave us our first direct experience turning ideas into functioning software prototypes while navigating strict time limits and intense collaboration.

Why I built it & Team Hackathon Journey

ROAD SOS initiated a long-term partnership with my team. Since that first competition, we have participated in more than 10 hackathons together and reached Stage 2 in more than two of them.

“We did not win, but working as a team helped me learn and grow through collaboration and shared problem-solving.”

We didn’t win, but the experience of working together under pressure taught me invaluable principles: rapid iteration, mutual trust, and how to break down complex challenges collaboratively. It established the collaborative mindset I carry into all my current software ventures.

Project links & Inquiries

ROAD SOS was engineered as an intensive hackathon prototype. If you are interested in discussing team problem-solving or hackathon collaborations, feel free to reach out.
